## 2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to operate the service. We do **not** collect data for advertising, and we do **not** sell your data to anyone.

### 2.1 App Customers (Qadir Food)

| Data type | Details | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile number | Used for registration and login via one-time password (OTP) | Account creation and authentication |
| Name | The name you enter at registration | Identifying you and fulfilling orders |
| Address & GPS location | Your coordinates and saved addresses | Determining and completing delivery |
| Order history | Details of your past and current orders | Service delivery, support, invoicing |
| Payment data | Processed directly via the Moyasar payment gateway | Completing payments |
| Device & notification data | Push token, device type | Sending order status updates |

> **Note on payments:** We do **not** store your full card details (card number/CVV) on our servers. This data is processed directly by the licensed, **PCI DSS**-compliant **Moyasar** payment gateway. We also offer **cash on delivery**.

### 2.2 Delivery Captains (Qadir Food Captain)

In addition to the above (mobile, name, location), we collect from captains:

| Data type | Details | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| National ID / Iqama number | To verify identity and work eligibility | Verification and legal compliance |
| Daily selfie | Face verification before starting a shift | Confirming captain identity, fraud prevention |
| Live GPS location | Captain location during shift and delivery | Order tracking, routing, delivery safety |
| Document photos | Any required documents (license/vehicle) | Eligibility verification |

> A captain's live location is collected **only during working hours/shifts** and for operational and safety purposes.

### 2.3 Restaurants/Merchants (Qadir Food Merchant)

Owner/responsible-staff data: mobile, name, store data (name in Arabic and English, location, menu, working hours), and financial settlement details.

### 2.4 Use of the Camera

We request **Camera** permission in the Captain app to capture the daily selfie and verification documents, and it may be used in the Customer/Merchant apps for optional purposes (e.g., a drop-off photo or menu images). We never capture images without an action you initiate.

We process your data based on one or more of the following legal bases under the PDPL:

1. **Performance of a contract/service:** to fulfill and deliver your order and manage your account.
2. **Your consent:** for certain processing such as location and notifications (you may withdraw it at any time).
3. **Legal obligation:** to comply with Saudi laws (e.g., retaining invoices and verifying captain identity).
4. **Legitimate interest:** to prevent fraud, ensure platform security, and improve service quality, without prejudice to your rights.

Primary purposes of processing:

- Account creation and OTP authentication.
- Receiving, processing, and delivering orders.
- Determining the delivery location and assigning the nearest captain.
- Processing payments via Moyasar or collecting cash.
- Sending order-status notifications and support messages.
- Verifying captain identity and delivery safety.
- Technical support, customer service, and dispute resolution.
- Legal and accounting compliance.

## 5. Data Encryption and Security

- All data is transmitted between the apps and our servers over an **encrypted (HTTPS/TLS)** connection.
- Passwords/secrets, where present, are stored hashed, and authentication is managed via secure tokens (JWT).
- We apply technical and administrative access controls to limit data access on a need-to-know basis.
- Sensitive payment data is processed by the PCI DSS-compliant Moyasar gateway and is not stored by us.

Despite reasonable measures, no system can be guaranteed 100% secure. We are committed to notifying the competent authority and affected individuals of any data-breach incident as required by the PDPL.

## 7. Your Rights Under the PDPL

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, you have the right to:

1. **Right to be informed:** know the legal basis and purpose of collecting your data.
2. **Right of access:** view your personal data held by us and obtain a copy.
3. **Right to rectification:** request correction, updating, or completion of inaccurate data.
4. **Right to erasure:** request deletion of your data when no longer needed (subject to legal obligations).
5. **Right to withdraw consent:** withdraw your consent to a given processing at any time (e.g., location or notifications).
6. **Right to data portability:** obtain your data in a structured, machine-readable format where applicable.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: **[Email]**. We will respond within the statutory period. You also have the right to file a complaint with the competent authority (SDAIA) if you believe our processing violates the law.
